Word: spin
Pronunciation: /spɪn/

Meanings:
[verb] to rotate around a central point or axis; to twirl or whirl; to cause to rotate rapidly; to produce by spinning, as in spinning thread; to give a particular direction or slant to, often in a biased or deceptive way
Examples:
1. The dancer spun gracefully around the stage.
2. He spun the wheel of fortune.
3. She spun a yarn about her travels.
4. The politician tried to spin the news to his advantage.
