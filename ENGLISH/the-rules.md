# Specific Identity 

Use "the" when a listener knows which specific entity you are referring to (if something has already been mentioned or it's just obvious).

*I adopted a cat. [some text] The cat is sleeping.*

# Unique Entities

Use "the" when there is only one of something in existence.

*The sun, the equator, the internet, the global population, the atmosphere, etc.*

# Collective Adjectives

Use "the" when referring to a demographic group of people.

*The elderly, the marginalized, the wealthy, the French.*

# Specific Geographical Names

Plural nations or regions: *The Netherlands, the Philippines.*
Nations with "States, Kingdom, or Republic": *The US, the UK, the Czech Republic.*
Bodies of water: *The Pacific Ocean, the Mediterranean Sea, the Amazon River.*
Mountains (but not individual peaks): *The Himalayas, the Alps.*

# Superlatives And Ordinals

*The greatest, the most effective, the least harmful.*
*The first, the second, the third, the fourth, and so on.*
