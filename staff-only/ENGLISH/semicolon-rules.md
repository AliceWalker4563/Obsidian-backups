# Connecting Independent Clauses

Use a semicolon if 2 clauses are independent, but closely related and there is no coordinating conjunction (FANBOYS). 

*I'm really hungry; I need to grab some food.*

# Same But With a Conjunctive Adverb

Use to connect 2 independent clauses, if the second begins with a conjunctive adverb (however, therefore, moreover, nevertheless, thus) or a transitional phrase (for example, as a result).

*You were such a good boy; therefore, here is your treat.*

# Separating Items In a Complex List

The semicolon acts like a more strong comma.

*The charity operates in Nairobi, Kenya; Kampala, Uganda; and Accra, Ghana.*
